Your trade journey, explained clearly

Not sure where to start?

Whether you are beginning an apprenticeship, preparing for a level exam, applying to challenge a certification exam, or studying for the Red Seal exam, this page gives you a clear starting path. Requirements differ by province, territory, and trade, so we also help you identify the right official next step.

Choose Your TradeAsk Us for Guidance
1

Identify your goal

Decide whether you need apprenticeship registration, technical training, a Level 1–4 exam, final certification, or a Red Seal endorsement. If you are unsure, send us your trade, province, experience, and current status.

2

Confirm eligibility officially

Contact your provincial or territorial apprenticeship authority. They confirm required work hours, school training, documents, fees, exam eligibility, challenge pathways, and any trade-specific rules.

3

Arrange training when required

Registered apprentices normally complete workplace hours and scheduled technical training. Experienced workers may qualify through a trade-qualifier or challenge route, depending on the jurisdiction and trade.

4

Prepare for the correct exam

Use the official occupational standard, training-provider materials, current codes, and our independent practice questions. Select level-exam practice for apprenticeship stages or full-trade practice for final certification preparation.

5

Apply, schedule, and write

Submit the required application and documents to the official authority. Once approved, follow its scheduling instructions, identification requirements, exam rules, and rewrite policy.

6

Keep moving after the result

If you pass, confirm the remaining certification steps. If you do not pass, review your score breakdown, focus on weak areas, verify the waiting period, and build a structured rewrite plan.

We will support you through the journey

Premium members can contact us for practical guidance throughout their preparation journey—including help understanding where to begin, which authority to contact, how level exams differ from final certification, what information to gather before applying, and how to organize a study plan. We provide independent guidance and study support; final eligibility and approval always come from the official provincial or territorial authority.
