What Are Apprenticeship Level Exams?

Level exams are usually written during apprenticeship training. They help confirm that you understand the trade knowledge taught at each stage before you continue toward certification and final Red Seal preparation.

Level 1

Foundation safety, tools, materials, basic calculations, trade terminology, and entry-level jobsite knowledge.

Level 2

Intermediate installation, service, troubleshooting, code awareness, diagrams, and applied trade theory.

Level 3

Advanced troubleshooting, diagnostics, system planning, code-style problem solving, and deeper applied trade theory.

Level 4

Final apprenticeship readiness, complex scenarios, supervision-level thinking, and transition into Red Seal review.

Level Exam FAQ

Are level exams the same as the Red Seal exam?

No. Level exams are usually part of apprenticeship training. The Red Seal exam is the final interprovincial certification exam for eligible trades.

Can I practice Level 1, Level 2, Level 3, and Level 4?

Yes. This section is designed to organize study material by level so users can prepare step by step.

Are these official government exam questions?

No. Red Seal Practice is an independent exam preparation website. Questions are Red Seal-style and level-style study questions, not official government exam questions.
