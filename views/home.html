Are these official exam questions?

No. These are independent Red Seal-style and level-style practice questions for study purposes.

Can I try questions for free?

Yes. Free trade pages include sample practice before users upgrade to premium access.

Are level exams different?

Yes. Level exams are part of apprenticeship training, while the Red Seal is the final certification exam.
